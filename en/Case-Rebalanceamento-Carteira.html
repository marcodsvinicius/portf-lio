Investment Portfolio Rebalancing

Retaining users and adding value to the B2B Home Broker. Creation of a new investment management feature that curbed user churn to third-party platforms, elevating the value of the product offered to financial market giants such as Nubank, C6 Bank, Sicoob, and Sicredi.

B2B2C User Retention Churn Benchmarking UX Strategy Wireframing

Company

Nelogica

My Role

Product Designer (UX/UI)

Impact Summary

Solved a critical churn problem by creating a native portfolio management ecosystem, keeping users engaged within the product instead of migrating to external tools or spreadsheets.

01 / SCENARIO

Context and Challenge

Nelogica's Home Broker is a robust analysis and trading platform, sold in a B2B model to massive financial institutions (like Nubank, C6 Bank, Sicoob, and Sicredi). During strategic discussions with the UX Leadership and Tech Lead, we identified a concerning behavioral pattern in the usage data.

Investors would enter the platform, learn about the market, and use indicators for study. However, over time, engagement plummeted. The reason? Financial maturity.

The Core Problem

We were suffering from a retention bottleneck (churn). Upon defining their long-term investment goals, users needed to "rebalance" their portfolios. Since our Home Broker lacked this continuous management tool, investors were abandoning our application and migrating to complex spreadsheets or third-party platforms (like SmarttInvest and StatusInvest) to manage their wealth.

02 / STRATEGY

My Role and Processes

  • Conducted an Adapted Discovery Due to strict compliance and data protection policies that prevented direct access to end-users, I structured an in-depth market research initiative, analyzing the behavior of long-term investors and the wealth rebalancing journey.
  • Mapped the Competitive Landscape I performed a critical benchmarking analysis against the top 3 solutions in the market (Spreadsheets, StatusInvest, and SmarttInvest). I identified that our greatest competitive advantage would be the automatic integration of buy and sell orders, something competitors required users to do manually.
  • Managed Expectations and Technical Feasibility I facilitated continuous alignment meetings with the UX Leadership (Renan Paz) and the Tech Lead. I translated business insights into technical requirements, ensuring the scope was feasible for the development team.
  • Architected the User Journey To organize the business rules, I created detailed flowcharts covering two crucial scenarios: Onboarding (first access and importing the pre-existing portfolio from the Home Broker) and Continuous Use (main management screen).
03 / SOLUTION

The New Experience

03 / SOLUTION

The New Experience

Cognitive Load Protection The Home Broker's main trading screen already had a very high information density. To avoid overwhelming (or confusing) the user, I designed the Rebalancing feature in a visually isolated and clean environment, 100% focused on chart analysis and goals.
"Invest Balance" Smart Feature In partnership with the Tech Lead, we designed a feature where the user inputs an available amount (e.g., R$ 1,000). The interface automatically calculates and suggests how to distribute this balance among assets to keep the portfolio perfectly aligned with the investor's goal.
Mobile-First Approach Since tracking wealth is a daily habit, I designed the entire interface prioritizing the mobile experience, ensuring clear chart visualization and easy inclusion/exclusion of assets in the account.
Visual Feedback and Flexibility Implementation of easy-to-read charts that compare the "Current Portfolio" with the "Investment Goal," along with allowing the user to disable specific assets from being accounted for in the rebalancing.

05 / TAKEAWAYS

Project Lessons

01 / STRATEGIC DESIGN AND RETENTION

Strategic design goes beyond creating new interfaces: it is about mapping and solving the business pains that mitigate user churn to third-party platforms.

02 / TECHNICAL AND UX SYNCHRONIZATION

Weekly alignments and continuous technical validations prevented rework and ensured that engineering deadlines were met without losing quality.

03 / INDIRECT DISCOVERY (B2B)

The lack of user contact (due to B2B compliance) taught us to use alternative methods like in-depth competitive benchmarking and empirical investor scenarios.
