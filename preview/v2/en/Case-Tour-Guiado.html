03

The challenge

The trading platform includes tools such as order entry, order lists and portfolio tracking. Guided tours needed to introduce these capabilities without packing too much information into one sequence.

The interface review revealed too many steps and long text blocks. Instructions appeared at the top of the screen, while the next button sat at the bottom, requiring attention to shift between two areas.

04

Design decisions

Organize learning around individual goals

Why this decision
The previous flow grouped too much information into long tutorials.
How I applied it
I organized the content into “Getting Started” and “Market Tools”, restructuring six tutorials into thirteen focused paths.

Bring instructions and controls together

Why this decision
Explanatory text and the next action occupied distant areas of the screen.
How I applied it
I redesigned the modals to place instructions near the controls and revised the copy to make each step more direct.

Work directly in high fidelity

Why this decision
The main scope concerned text layout and the division of steps.
How I applied it
Based on the audit and benchmarking, I worked directly in high fidelity to discuss content and interface adjustments.

07

Lessons learned

Content is part of the interface

The work involved dividing and organizing instructions as well as designing modals.

Decisions tied to the problem

Bringing text and controls together addressed friction identified in the interface review.

Learning in stages

Focused paths organized onboarding around smaller goals and offered continuity at the end of each tour.
