03

The challenge

The trading platform was offered to financial institutions through a B2B model. Discussions with UX leadership and the Tech Lead highlighted the needs of investors moving from trading to tracking long-term goals.

Without native rebalancing, portfolio management depended on spreadsheets or external services. The challenge was to incorporate this capability while accounting for the platform’s complexity and limited access to users.

04

Design decisions

Adapt discovery to access constraints

Why this decision
Compliance policies limited direct research with investors.
How I applied it
I analyzed spreadsheets, StatusInvest and SmarttInvest, organized usage scenarios and aligned requirements with UX and engineering. This supported solution definition without replacing user testing.

Separate portfolio management from trading

Why this decision
The trading screen already contained dense information and many actions.
How I applied it
I designed a dedicated rebalancing space with charts comparing current allocation and investment goals.

Address first use and ongoing management

Why this decision
The experience needed to cover both initial access and recurring tasks.
How I applied it
I mapped onboarding, existing portfolio import and ongoing management. Together with the technical lead, I designed the “Invest Balance” feature.

The solution

Deliverables and evidence

Defined flows

The solution covers first access, portfolio import and ongoing management.

A capability within the product

The design brings portfolio tracking and adjustment planning into the trading platform.

Technical alignment

Scope decisions were discussed with UX leadership and the Tech Lead throughout the project.

Retention was a project objective. The available material does not include measurements that attribute reduced churn to this feature; technical reviews are also distinct from usability testing.

07

Lessons learned

Research under constraints

Benchmarking and usage scenarios helped structure the proposal within limits on user access.

Feasibility during design

Engineering collaboration informed feature definition and business rules.

Space for different tasks

Portfolio management received its own experience within a platform also built for trading.
